Overview & Quick Facts
Who is Virat Kohli beyond the match scorecards.
Profile • Basics • Role

Virat Kohli (born 5 November 1988 in Delhi, India) is a right-handed top-order batter, former captain of the Indian cricket team, and one of the greatest modern-day run machines.

Famous as the “Chase Master”, he’s known for his unreal consistency while chasing, unmatched fitness, and intensity that lifts the entire team.

Born 5 Nov 1988 • Delhi
Batting Style Right-handed • Top Order
Bowling Right-arm medium (occasional)
Teams India • Delhi • RCB/RCBengaluru

From leading India to historic Test wins overseas to owning the ODI record books, Kohli has inspired a generation of cricketers with his professionalism, mental toughness, and passion for the game.
